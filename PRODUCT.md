## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: owners and decision-makers at B2B and commercial businesses in Australia that want more commercial work. Examples include commercial contractors and grounds maintenance, property and facilities service providers, specialist B2B service businesses, and professional service and consultancy firms. They arrive evaluating whether Versflows can give them a more consistent way to reach commercial buyers, partnerships or larger contracts.

Secondary: established local service businesses (landscapers, pool builders and other trades) that want more project enquiries.

## Product Purpose
Versflows Acquisitions is a customer acquisition and lead generation agency. It builds and runs structured, repeatable acquisition systems: targeted prospect research, verified decision-maker databases, personalised outbound campaigns, follow-up, reply qualification, and CRM handoff with reporting. Success for the site is enquiries from suitable B2B businesses via "Discuss Your Growth Goals".

## Positioning
A managed outbound service: not a list vendor, not software access, not a generic full-service marketing agency, not exclusively a cold-email or landscaping agency, not a web design agency. Language is "structured", "more consistent", "more predictable". It never guarantees leads, contracts or revenue.

## Operating Context
- Clients provide their market knowledge, examples of good clients, and approval of targeting and messaging. Versflows handles everything else, then hands qualified opportunities into a CRM (GoHighLevel).
- Campaigns comply with the Spam Act 2003.
- The secondary local offer covers Meta Ads, websites and landing pages, local lead generation, CRM setup and follow-up automation.

- Markwicks Services (commercial maintenance and grounds, Bathurst/Orange/Lithgow NSW): an outbound campaign in preparation, shown only as an "Example of our process".
- Absent and must not be fabricated: metrics, revenue figures, testimonials, endorsements, case-study outcomes, team members, company history, credentials.

## Product Principles
1. B2B lead generation is the obvious primary offer. Local services stay visibly secondary.
2. Show the process, not promises. The mechanism is the proof.
3. Every claim must be true today.
4. Clarity over persuasion tricks: no urgency gimmicks or fake scarcity.
