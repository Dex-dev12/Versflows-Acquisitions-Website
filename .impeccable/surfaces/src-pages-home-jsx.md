## Scope and mode

The whole marketing site (Home, B2B Lead Generation, Additional Services, About, Contact, legal). The visitor mode is Persuade. The primary action is "Discuss Your Growth Goals", which goes to /contact. The direction is pinned by the user: a dark site modelled on leftclick.ai's dark mode, with no image in the hero. The copy carries over from the light version. The build is code-led, because no image generation is available.

## Audience and proof

B2B and commercial owners and decision-makers in Australia, with local service businesses as a secondary audience. The only proof available is a client logo strip (Ground Up, Fibonacci Landscapes, Markwicks, First Impressions) and the Markwicks process example. No metrics may be shown.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The mechanism is the proof. A dark, quiet room where one lit pipeline shows a prospect travelling from market to CRM handoff. It refuses the dark-agency default: a neon glow, a stat row of invented numbers, and a grid of identical icon cards.

OWN-WORLD:
- A near-black ground #0A0C11, with one soft navy radial glow (#0B2247) behind the hero and the closing call to action.
- Bone-white text #F0F2F5, with secondary text at 62% white.
- Translucent panels: 4% white fill, a hairline 8% white border, 18px radius.
- White pill buttons with a round signal-blue arrow button (#0060FC); ghost pills for secondary actions.
- Geist 500 for display with tight tracking. No eyebrows and no monospace labels.
- Logo blue appears only in the brand mark, the pipeline's light and the arrow buttons.

STORY: The visitor sees what Versflows does (builds a predictable pipeline) and watches the mechanism run. They see real businesses' logos, understand that B2B outbound is the core offer and local work is secondary, and get one consistent action.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- A centred headline at about 72px desktop, across two lines, with one line of support copy.
- Below it, the primary white pill and a ghost "Explore Our Process".
- Under the buttons, a full-width pipeline track in a translucent panel. Six stations run Market → Handoff, with a blue light travelling between them and each station's label brightening as it passes. The panel carries a small "Illustrative workflow" caption.
- The logo strip band starts at the fold.

FORM: pinned brief (leftclick.ai dark-mode grammar) translated: their stat row becomes our pipeline instrument. seed: not rolled. Direction pinned by the user ("it should look like leftclick.ai … dark and background like this site, not the white on the current site"), which beats the roll, so concept-seed was not run.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
